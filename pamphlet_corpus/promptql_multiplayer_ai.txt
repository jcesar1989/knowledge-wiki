# PromptQL: Multiplayer AI. Shared Context

**promptql**

## Page 1

PromptQL gives your team AI co-workers for real work. Start a bot for a question, a task or an ongoing job. Invite teammates and work through it together.

Connect your databases and everyday tools. Bots analyze data, call APIs, run code and create dashboards, sheets, docs, decks and boards.

As you work, review proposed updates to a shared wiki. Decisions, definitions and corrections become context other bots can use.

### The problem

A great AI demo doesn't automatically become a way of working. Teams still hit a familiar pattern:

AI can speed up individual tasks. Teamwork still slows down when the right data, expertise and decisions are scattered across tools and conversations.

PromptQL brings people, bots and shared context into the work itself. Less re-explaining. More to build on.

### Move faster, *together.*

Give a bot a job. Bring in the people who know the details. Turn the conversation into work your team can review, improve and use.

**Data and system access**
Connect databases, SaaS tools and APIs instead of manually assembling every input. Bots use the data and tools the person asking is allowed to access.

**Knowledge silos**
Invite teammates to the same bot to clarify a definition, correct an assumption or add an exception. Review wiki updates so that knowledge helps the next person, too.

**Expertise gap**
Go beyond an answer. Build a dashboard, update a sheet, draft a doc, assemble a deck or turn a meeting into a board with owners and next steps.

**Translation tax**
Work through the details with the bot and your teammates in one conversation. Review the output, give feedback and improve it without starting the handoff over.

### Technical innovations

- **Operational wiki.** Shared knowledge for your business. Capture definitions, decisions and exceptions. Review and refine them as work changes.
- **Code-backed execution.** Bots run code to query data, calculate and carry out tasks. Inspect the work and reuse programs for repeatable jobs.
- **Connected data access.** Query connected sources without a new warehouse. Save outputs or materialize data when the workflow calls for it.
- **Teachable in the flow.** Correct assumptions and explain exceptions. Review proposed wiki updates to control what becomes shared knowledge.
- **Multiplayer design.** One bot, multiple collaborators. Invite people to review the work, add context and move it forward together.
- **Permissions and governance.** Bots use your permissions; participants see outputs. Configure access and scopes. Dedicated infra with Enterprise.
