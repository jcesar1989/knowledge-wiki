## What the AI gateway enforces on every call

- **Human & agent access:** RBAC by role, group, tool, and action, with a dedicated identity for every agent.
- **Runtime policy:** Allow, deny, or require approval per tool call and action, enforced inline.
- **AI-ready DLP:** PII and secrets masked or blocked before they reach a model or MCP server.
- **Routing & fallback:** Frontier and self-hosted models, routed to the most cost-effective option.
- **Usage & cost control:** Real-time spend and hard caps by user, team, agent, and model.
- **Observability & audit:** Every action and the policy behind it, streamed to Splunk or Datadog.

# Create and run reliable AI automations

Describe a process and Barndoor turns it into defined steps that run the same way every time.

[Diagram: Four-step process flow with check-mark nodes connected by dashed lines: "Deterministic" (No model involved); "Scoped inference" (AI judgment, fenced in); "Validated" (Every output checked to schema); "Conditional" (Control flow you can read).]

- **Precise:** Schema-validated output on every run.
- **Scoped:** Inference only when and where you define it.
- **Fast:** Built in natural language. No nodes to wire.
