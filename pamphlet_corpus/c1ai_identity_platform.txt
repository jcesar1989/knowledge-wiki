## Identity is growing faster than governance.

Every new employee, service account, and AI agent adds access that someone has to review, own, and eventually remove. AI is only as valuable as the access and context it can act on. Most organizations can't deliver the right access to every identity, across every system, at the speed the business needs. Security, IT, and identity teams add a new integration for every new agent or workflow. Context fragments, access reviews take weeks, and standing privileges pile up. The risk is real: 80% of organizations experienced at least one identity-related breach in the past year.¹

AI adoption compounds the problem. Agents have no identity. Credentials leak into agent context. Nothing enforces policy at runtime. Shadow AI is already running. And the human workforce problem never went away. Without a governed path that moves at enterprise speed, teams work around governance entirely.

### What C1.ai delivers

**Complete visibility**
Every person, service account, workload, and agent, linked to its accounts, entitlements, and accountable owners. Risky access gets found and fixed before it becomes a security issue.

**One connected inventory**
Findings, governance, agent access, credentials, and work run on the same identity and access data. People and agents are governed under one policy and one audit trail.

**Built for engineers**
Automate through the REST API and SDKs, the C1.ai CLI, Terraform, and Functions. Access policy lives in code, so every change is reviewed, versioned, and repeatable.

## One platform. The full identity stack.

The C1 Identity Platform connects data from your systems into one shared identity and access inventory. Every finding, review, grant, and agent action draws on the same data, so security sees the full picture, IT stops stitching tools together, and governance keeps pace as the business adds people, systems, and agents.

For governance, that means automated joiner-mover-leaver workflows, just-in-time access in place of standing privilege, and AI-assisted access reviews, so teams can modernize identity without rebuilding it. For AI, agents get their own scoped identities instead of borrowed human ones, inference is routed by risk and cost, and agent keys stay in a vault, out of code and context. An open-source connector ecosystem extends coverage to any system.
